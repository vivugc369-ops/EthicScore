The Big Truth: Who Really Owns Your Skincare Brand?

You might think you're buying from a small, ethical brand. But most "indie" brands are actually owned by massive conglomerates.

Here are the biggest parent companies:

L'Oréal owns:

  • CeraVe
  • La Roche-Posay
  • Youth To The People
  • Garnier
  • Vichy

Estée Lauder owns:

  • The Ordinary
  • Drunk Elephant
  • Clinique
  • MAC

Unilever owns:

  • Simple
  • Dove
  • Pond's

Johnson & Johnson owns:

  • Neutrogena
  • Aveeno

Shiseido owns:

  • Drunk Elephant (sold to them)

Why does this matter?

When you buy from a brand owned by a conglomerate, your money goes to a company that may test on animals or use unethical practices.
