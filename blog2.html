How to Read a Skincare Ingredient Label Like a Pro

Skincare ingredient lists can look like a chemistry textbook. Here is how to decode them:

Step 1: Check the First 3 Ingredients

These make up 80% of the product. If water is first, the product is mostly water.

Step 2: Look for Active Ingredients

  • Vitamin C – Brightens skin
  • Retinol – Anti-aging
  • Niacinamide – Reduces redness
  • Hyaluronic Acid – Hydrates

Step 3: Avoid These Harmful Ingredients

  • Parabens – Linked to hormone disruption
  • Sulfates – Strip natural oils
  • Phthalates – Linked to health issues
  • Formaldehyde – Known irritant

Step 4: Check for Cruelty-Free Certifications

Look for "Leaping Bunny" or "Cruelty-Free" logos.

Step 5: Find the Parent Company

Many "natural" brands are owned by giant corporations. Use EthicScore to check who really owns your product.
